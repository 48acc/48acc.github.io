Transport

Monash University is 25km south-east of the centre of Melbourne. Monash University has several campuses. 48ACC is at the main campus, called "Clayton campus".

Direct transport from airport

Melbourne airport is about 20km north-west of the centre of Melbourne, on the opposite side of the city to Monash University. A taxi from the airport to Monash University is approximately $120.

Public transport from airport to Melbourne city

All public transport options from the airport to Monash go through central Melbourne. For travel from the airport to central Melbourne, take a taxi (approximately $60) or the skybus ($25.90 each way). There is no train to the airport. The skybus takes you to Southern Cross Station at the western end of central Melbourne.

Public transport from Melbourne city to Monash

Go to the Town Hall station or the State Library station, and take the Pakenham Line or Cranbourne Line train, and get off at Huntingdale Station. (You can get from Southern Cross Station to Town Hall station via a train to Flinders Street Station and a short underground walk through `Flinders Link'.) From Huntingdale, take a bus to Monash (route 601, 630 or 900). The 601 runs every 5 minutes for most of the day.

Payment options

There are several ways to pay for public transport in Melbourne:

  • purchase a myki card ($6) and then add money to the card
  • use the PTV app on your mobile phone
  • tap your Visa or Mastercard card to pay instantly
  • tap your contactless payment card in your phone's digital wallet
Myki can be used on trains, trams and buses (but not the skybus to and from the airport). For more information about public transport in Melbourne, see the Public Transport Victoria website. Their journey planner is very helpful.

Car Parking

Visitors to Monash can park their car in the ground floor of the N1 car park near the workshop venue for $21.30 per day.
