Student Prize

The CMSA Anne Penfold Street Student Prize is awarded to the student who is judged to have given the best presentation at the ACC. If you answer "Yes" to the "Should your talk be considered for the Anne Penfold Street prize?" question during registration, you will automatically be considered for the student prize. If you have any questions about eligibility for the award, please email the organisers at acc48acc@gmail.com.

Student Support

A limited amount of travel funding from the CMSA is available for students who attend the ACC and present a talk, and who reside outside of Melbourne. Note that applications for students based at institution in Australia or New Zealand will be given priority, but those from students at other institutions will be considered. To apply for this funding, please email a letter of support from your supervisor, an approximate budget of your expenses, and a list of any other support that has been obtained or applied for from other sources. To be eligible for this funding, send these to sara.davies@uq.edu.au by 1 September 2026 with subject line CMSA Student Support.

Student Accommodation

A whatsapp group has been set up for students who are interested in sharing accommodation to reduce costs. If you are interested in joining this group, then email acc48acc "at" gmail.com with subject line "whatsapp group".
